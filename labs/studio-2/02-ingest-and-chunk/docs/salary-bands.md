# Salary Bands 2026 (HR only)

Fictional document for a course lab. All numbers are invented. This document is restricted to the HR group; it is in
the lab so you can test that retrieval never shows it to anyone else.

## Store roles

Sales associates are paid between 18 and 23 dollars per hour. Senior associates are paid between 22 and 27 dollars per
hour. Store managers are salaried between 62,000 and 78,000 dollars per year.

## Warehouse roles

Pickers and packers are paid between 19 and 24 dollars per hour, with a 2 dollar shift premium for the evening shift.
Warehouse leads are paid between 25 and 31 dollars per hour.

## Review cycle

Bands are reviewed every February against regional market data. Changes take effect on April 1.
