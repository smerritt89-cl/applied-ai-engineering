HARBOR & PINE TRAVEL POLICY

Fictional document for a course lab. This policy covers travel for store visits, trade shows, and supplier meetings.

Book flights and hotels through the company travel account so that bookings are visible to finance. Economy class is
the default for flights under six hours. For longer flights, premium economy is allowed with your manager's approval.
Hotels should cost no more than 180 dollars per night in most cities and 260 dollars per night in high-cost cities
such as New York, San Francisco, and Boston.

Meals are reimbursed up to 75 dollars per day with receipts. Alcohol is not reimbursed unless you are hosting a supplier
or customer, in which case note the guests' names on the expense report.

Mileage for personal cars is reimbursed at the rate finance publishes each January. Rental cars should be compact or
midsize unless you are moving display equipment.

Submit travel expenses within 30 days of returning. Late submissions need a short explanation and a director's
approval. If plans change, cancel bookings as early as you can; unused nonrefundable tickets should be reported to
finance so the credit is not lost.
