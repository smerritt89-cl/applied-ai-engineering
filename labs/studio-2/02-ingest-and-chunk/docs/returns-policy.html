Customer returns policy

Fictional document for a course lab. This page tells store and support staff how to handle returns.

Standard returns

Customers may return unused items within 60 days with a receipt. Refund to the original payment method. Without a receipt, offer store credit at the lowest sale price from the last 90 days.

Used gear

Used gear can be returned within 30 days if it failed in normal use, for example a tent pole that snapped or a zipper that split. Do not accept returns of gear that was clearly damaged by misuse. When in doubt, take photos and ask a store manager.

Online orders

Online orders can be returned by mail with the prepaid label in the box, or in any store. Support agents can issue a new label from the order page if the customer lost theirs.
