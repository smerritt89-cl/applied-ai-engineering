# Harbor & Pine Outfitters Employee Handbook

This handbook is a fictional document written for a course lab. Harbor & Pine is an invented outdoor-gear retailer with
three stores and a small warehouse.

## Working hours

### Store schedules

Store shifts are posted every Thursday for the following week. Swap requests go through your store manager at least
48 hours before the shift. Weekend shifts rotate so that nobody works more than two weekends in a row.

### Warehouse schedules

The warehouse runs two shifts, 6:00 to 14:30 and 14:00 to 22:30. The half-hour overlap is for handover: the outgoing
lead walks the incoming lead through open orders, damaged stock, and anything waiting on a carrier.

## Time off

### Vacation

Full-time staff accrue 1.25 vacation days per month in their first two years and 1.75 days per month after that.
Request vacation in the scheduling app at least three weeks ahead for peak season (November and December) and at
least one week ahead otherwise.

### Sick days

You do not need a doctor's note for absences of up to three consecutive days. Tell your manager before your shift
starts, by phone or in the team chat. Sick days do not reduce your vacation balance.

## Equipment and safety

Staff discounts on gear are 30 percent, or 40 percent on items you use for work, such as boots and rain shells.
Warehouse staff must wear steel-toe boots and high-visibility vests on the floor. Report any injury, however minor,
to the shift lead the same day so it can be logged.

## Expenses

Keep receipts for anything you buy for the company. Submit them in the expenses app within 30 days. Purchases over
200 dollars need approval from your manager before you buy. For travel, see the travel policy.
