# Northwind Field Services: Customer Support Handbook (fictional, for lab use)

Version 4.2. Owner: Support Operations. This handbook is the single source of truth for how support agents answer
customers about service plans, appointments, billing, and warranties. If a customer's question isn't covered here,
escalate to a team lead rather than improvising a policy.

## 1. Service plans

Northwind offers three residential service plans. Prices are per month and billed in advance on the first business day
of each month.

- **Basic** ($19 per month): one scheduled maintenance visit per year for a single heating or cooling system, phone
  support during business hours (8:00 to 18:00 local time, Monday to Friday), and a 10 percent discount on parts.
- **Plus** ($39 per month): two maintenance visits per year for up to two systems, phone and chat support seven days a
  week from 7:00 to 21:00, a 15 percent discount on parts, and no call-out fee for repairs during business hours.
- **Premier** ($69 per month): unlimited repair visits for up to three systems, 24-hour emergency support, a 20 percent
  discount on parts, priority scheduling (a technician within 24 hours for emergencies, 72 hours otherwise), and one
  free indoor air quality check per year.

Customers may upgrade at any time; the new price applies from the next billing date and the new benefits apply
immediately. Downgrades take effect on the next billing date. A customer may downgrade at most once in any 12-month
period. Plan changes requested in the last three days of a month are processed in the following month.

Commercial customers are not covered by this handbook. Transfer them to the Commercial Accounts team.

## 2. Appointments

### 2.1 Booking windows

Standard appointments are booked in four-hour windows: 8:00 to 12:00, 12:00 to 16:00, and 16:00 to 20:00. Premier
customers may request a two-hour window, subject to availability. Agents must not promise a specific arrival time
inside a window.

### 2.2 Rescheduling and cancellation

Customers can reschedule or cancel without charge up to 24 hours before the start of the window. Inside 24 hours, a
$35 late-change fee applies to Basic customers. Plus and Premier customers are not charged a late-change fee, but
after three late changes in a calendar year their next late change is charged at $35.

If a technician arrives and cannot access the property (no one home, locked gate, unsafe conditions), the visit is
recorded as a missed appointment. Missed appointments are charged $50 for all plans. Agents may waive one missed
appointment fee per customer per year if the customer reports a genuine emergency; record the reason in the account
notes.

### 2.3 Emergencies

An emergency is: no heat when the outdoor temperature is below 5 degrees Celsius, no cooling when it is above 32
degrees Celsius and the household includes someone over 70, under 2, or with a documented medical need, a gas smell,
or water leaking from equipment onto electrical fittings. For a gas smell, tell the customer to leave the building and
call the gas utility's emergency line first; do not book a visit until they confirm they are safe.

Premier customers get emergency dispatch at no charge. Basic and Plus customers may book an emergency visit for a $120
call-out fee plus parts and labor at their plan discount.

## 3. Billing

### 3.1 Payment methods and failed payments

We accept debit and credit cards and direct debit. If a payment fails, the system retries after three days and again
after seven days. After the second failed retry the plan is suspended: scheduled maintenance visits are paused, but
emergency support for Premier customers continues for 14 days. A suspended plan is reactivated within one business day
of a successful payment. No late fee is charged for the first failed payment in a 12-month period; after that, a $10
late fee applies.

### 3.2 Refunds

Monthly plan fees are refundable only in these cases:

- The customer cancels within 14 days of first signing up and has had no visits. Refund the full first month.
- A scheduled visit was missed by Northwind (the technician did not arrive in the window and did not call). Credit one
  month's plan fee to the account. Credits are not paid out as cash unless the customer closes the account.
- A billing error by Northwind. Refund the incorrect amount in full to the original payment method within 5 business
  days.

Parts and labor charges are refundable only through the warranty process in section 4. Agents can issue credits up to
$100 without approval; larger credits need a team lead.

### 3.3 Price changes

We give at least 30 days' notice before a plan price change. Customers who cancel within 30 days of the notice are not
charged any fee for leaving, even if they are within a minimum term.

## 4. Warranties

### 4.1 Workmanship warranty

All repairs carry a 90-day workmanship warranty. If the same fault recurs within 90 days of the repair, the return
visit, labor, and any parts we replace are free, on every plan. The customer must report the recurrence within the
90 days; the visit itself may happen later.

### 4.2 Parts warranty

Parts we supply carry the manufacturer's warranty, typically 12 months. During the parts warranty, the part is free;
labor is charged at the plan rate unless the workmanship warranty also applies. Premier customers pay no labor on
warranty part replacements.

### 4.3 Exclusions

Warranties do not cover damage from power surges, flooding, pests, or work done by anyone other than a Northwind
technician after our repair. They also do not cover consumables such as filters, which are sold separately.

## 5. Escalation and tone

Escalate to a team lead when a customer asks for a credit over $100, threatens legal action, reports a safety incident
involving a technician, or has contacted us three or more times about the same unresolved issue. When escalating,
tell the customer who will contact them and by when (a team lead responds within one business day).

Write in plain language. Give the specific answer first, then the reason, then the next step. Do not quote internal
section numbers to customers. Never guess at a policy; if the handbook doesn't say, say you'll check and escalate.

## 6. Frequently confused cases

- A Plus customer who reschedules inside 24 hours for the fourth time in a year is charged $35 for that change.
- A Basic customer with no heat at 2 degrees Celsius outside is an emergency, but pays the $120 call-out fee.
- A repair that fails again after 95 days is outside the workmanship warranty; parts may still be under the parts
  warranty.
- A customer who downgraded in March cannot downgrade again until March of the following year.
- Credits for a missed visit go to the account balance; they are only paid out when the account is closed.
