Tallow & Pine Outfitters: returns and orders policy (excerpt)

1. Unused items with original tags can be returned within 30 days of delivery.
2. Used items cannot be returned. Items that are defective are covered by a 1-year warranty: we repair or replace them.
3. Items marked "final sale" cannot be returned or exchanged.
4. Refunds go to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after we receive the return.
5. Exchanges ship free. For refunds, a $6 return label fee is deducted from the refund.
6. Orders can be cancelled within 1 hour of being placed. After that, the order ships and must be returned instead.
7. Items received as gifts can be returned for store credit only.
