# Tallyhub help center (excerpt used by the support bot)

Tallyhub is a fictional invoicing app for small businesses. This excerpt is what the support bot
retrieves from. Use it as the ground truth when you annotate traces.

## Plans and pricing
- Starter: $12 per month. Pro: $29 per month (raised from $24 on 2026-09-01; existing customers keep $24 until their next renewal after 2026-12-01).
- Annual billing costs the same as 10 months.

## Billing settings
- Settings > Billing: change plan, cancel, update the billing email.
- There is no free trial of Pro in the current plan information.

## Refunds and cancellation
- Annual plans can be refunded in full within 14 days of purchase.
- Monthly plans are not refundable. Customers can cancel any time; access continues to the end of the paid month.

## Integrations
- Supported: QuickBooks Online, Xero, Stripe, PayPal.
- Not supported: QuickBooks Desktop, cryptocurrency payments.

## Features
- Recurring invoices: Invoices > Recurring > New schedule.
- Export: Reports > Export. Choose CSV or PDF and filter by client or date range.
- Branding: Settings > Branding to upload a logo and pick colors.
- Tax: one default tax rate on Starter; per-line-item tax rates on Pro.
- Multi-currency invoices: Pro only.
- Payment reminders: Settings > Reminders. (Reminders are emails; there are no automatic late fees.)
- Attachments: use Add attachment when editing an invoice (PDF, up to 10 MB).
- Duplicate an invoice: open it, then More > Duplicate.
- Edit a sent invoice: open it and choose Edit; the client gets the updated version.
- Filter invoices by status (Draft, Sent, Unpaid, Overdue, Paid) on the Invoices page.
- Import clients: Clients > Import, upload a CSV.
- Not available: SMS sending, approval workflows, a public API, AutoPay, automatic duplicate merging.
- The mobile app needs an internet connection. There is no offline mode.

## Always hand off to a person
Offer to connect the customer with the support team (and do not try to resolve it yourself) for:
account lockouts, chargebacks or payment disputes, data deletion or privacy (GDPR) requests,
legal threats, and suspected fraud.
