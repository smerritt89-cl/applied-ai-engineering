# Vendor summary: Quillmark (push notifications)

This is a plain-language summary of the contract for the project team. The signed contract is the
authoritative version; ask legal before relying on any clause.

## Contract basics

- Vendor: Quillmark Ltd (fictional)
- Service: push notifications for iOS and Android, UK data residency
- Start date: 1 February 2026
- Expiry: 31 January 2027
- Price: a fixed monthly platform fee plus a per-message charge above the included volume

## Renewal and notice

- The contract does not renew automatically.
- To renew or renegotiate, Fernhill must give written notice at least 60 days before expiry,
  that is, by 2 December 2026.
- If notice is late, Quillmark may treat the renewal as a new contract at list price, which is about
  30% higher than the current rate.
- A renewal can be for 12 or 24 months.

## Service levels

- Monthly availability target: 99.9%
- Service credits apply below the target, capped at one month's platform fee.

## Exit

- On expiry without renewal, Quillmark keeps the service running for 30 days in read-only mode
  (no new messages), which would break launch criterion 3.
