# Larkspur launch plan

Project Larkspur is Fernhill Analytics' new field-technician scheduling app.
Fernhill Analytics and everything in this folder are fictional, written for this lab.

## Summary

- Product owner: Priya Raman
- Engineering lead: Marcus Lee
- Launch date: 15 February 2027 (moved from 20 January 2027; see decision D-14)
- Launch markets: United Kingdom and Ireland
- Pricing: per technician seat, monthly billing

## Launch criteria

The launch goes ahead only if all of the following hold on 1 February 2027:

1. Crash-free sessions are at or above 99.5% over the previous 14 days.
2. Single sign-on works with the two pilot customers' identity providers.
3. Push notifications are under a signed contract for the full first year.
4. Support has a staffed rota for launch week.
5. No open risk in the risk register is rated High.

## Dependencies

- Single sign-on depends on the identity team's work, tracked in risk R-2.
- Push notifications depend on the Quillmark contract, tracked in risk R-3.
- The support rota depends on hiring two contractors, tracked in risk R-6.

## Communications

Marketing will announce the date publicly only after the app store submission is accepted.
Internal teams should treat 15 February 2027 as the working date, not as a public commitment.
