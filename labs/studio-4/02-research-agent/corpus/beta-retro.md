# Closed beta retrospective, 21 October 2026

Six weeks of closed beta with 3 customers and 48 technicians.

## What went well

- Offline mode worked in every site visit we observed, which validated decision D-07.
- Technicians adopted the job checklist feature faster than expected.
- Support tickets per technician fell each week after week two.

## What did not go well

- Older Android devices crashed when opening large job attachments (risk R-1, since fixed).
- Push notifications were delayed by up to 20 minutes during one Quillmark incident on 2 October.
- Two customers asked for single sign-on before they would roll out beyond the pilot.

## Numbers

- Crash-free sessions: 98.9% in week one, 99.7% by week six.
- Median time to complete a job record: 4 minutes 10 seconds.
- Net promoter score from technicians: +31.

## Changes we agreed

1. Fix the attachment crash before any further rollout (done in build 0.9.4).
2. Ask Quillmark for an incident report on 2 October and add alerting on notification delay.
3. Treat single sign-on as a launch criterion, not a nice-to-have.
